Scheduled messages were rebuilt, the Ultimate plan launched, and buttons can now post their reply in another channel.

<!--truncate-->

- **Rebuilt scheduled messages.** Schedules can repeat every few minutes, hours, days, weeks, or months, or go out on a list of dates. You set them up as a sentence with a preview, see them on a calendar, and can send a test before saving. See [Scheduled messages](/features/scheduled-messages).
- **One free recurring message.** Free servers can now have 1 recurring scheduled message. More of them need Embed Generator Premium.
- **Why a schedule failed.** The list of scheduled messages shows why the last send failed or why a schedule was turned off. Messages that Discord rejects aren't retried anymore.
- **Publish in announcement channels.** A scheduled message in an announcement channel can be published to the servers that follow it.
- **Ultimate plan.** Ultimate costs $9.99 a month per server and has everything in Premium with higher limits, like 500 saved messages and 100 scheduled messages. When you reach a limit, the app now shows what the next plan includes. See [Premium](/docs/premium).
- **Earlier versions of saved messages.** Overwriting a saved message keeps the old version, and History lets you preview and restore it. Premium keeps 5 versions of each message and Ultimate keeps 50. See [Saved messages](/docs/features/save-messages).
- **Send to another channel.** Buttons, select menus, and commands can post their reply in another channel, like a report that goes to a staff channel. Replies can also be built right in the action instead of picking a saved message. Both need Premium.
- **Open Discohook links.** Links with `?data=` from Embed Generator or Discohook open in the editor. Change the domain of a Discohook link to message.style and it opens here. See [Message Links](/docs/guides/message-links).
- **Up to 40 components.** Components V2 messages can hold 40 components, the same as Discord allows. Imported messages with layouts open in Components V2 mode.
- **Timestamps in variables.** `discordTimestamp` turns a time into a Discord timestamp that every reader sees in their own timezone. See [Message Variables](/docs/guides/variables).
- **Channel picker hints.** A greyed out channel now shows whether you or the bot is missing Manage Webhooks there, with a link to the fix. See [Channel Permissions](/docs/guides/channel-permissions).
- **Smaller fixes.** Invalid messages get an error that says what's wrong. Embed timestamps are checked in the editor. Inline code keeps its spaces in the preview. Sending after switching servers no longer goes to a channel of the server you had picked before.

Try it all in the [editor](https://message.style/app/editor).
